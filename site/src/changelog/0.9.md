For this release, we moved two real sites into ssqch: opensource.guide, from Jekyll, and expressjs.com, from Astro. Nothing finds the roots on a trail like hauling a couple of big sites down it, so most of what's new is something one of them tripped over on the way.

## The headlines

- **Jekyll sites feel at home.** Liquid's math filters and `split` work the way Ruby's Liquid does, `{% include_relative %}` works, and a Jekyll plugin's tag can move into your config as a function. Run `ssqch docs --from jekyll` to see the whole route before you start walking.
- **Your laptop and CI agree on the date.** Set `timeZone: "UTC"` and `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`, and your dates stop depending on where the build happened to run. v8.dev had 137 pages whose dates changed with the time zone!
- **Spot the shifty bits before a move.** Hand `ssqch diff` three or more builds that ought to match, and it lists what varies between them. It's like comparing three blurry photos of the same creature, except you get answers.
- **Liquid lookups got quick.** A layout that searches a collection with `where` on every page builds 8.6 times faster. opensource.guide's port went from 6.8 s to 1.4 s.

## What you might need to change

- **Heading ids** follow GitHub's rules character for character now, so a heading with an accent, an emoji or a superscript can get a new id. Check your links to those anchors, or give the heading an id of its own (`## x² {#x2}`).
- **Pages without a date** sort after the dated ones, in path order. If you liked the old order, give those pages dates.
- **Liquid's `split`** drops empty strings at the end (`"a,b,,"` is two items), and a block that only sets variables no longer leaves blank lines behind.
- **A `computed` value named `page`** is an error now. It used to vanish without a word, which is a Sasquatch's job, not your data's. Give it another name.
- **`slot` is a container name** now. If you already have a container called `slot`, rename it.
