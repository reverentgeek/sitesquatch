SiteSquatch's first release, 0.2.0, came out on 8 November 2026, inspired by the best features of the most popular frameworks and set on a trail of its own. Most of what makes ssqch what it is today showed up in the releases that followed, up to 0.7.1, including the ranger in your terminal.

## The headlines

- **0.3: bring your own templates.** Liquid, Nunjucks and Jinja, Handlebars and Mustache joined in, and `templateEngineOverride` became `render`.
- **0.4: ssqch found its voice.** The deadpan park ranger moved into your build log, along with `base` for a site served under a path, `ssqch import eleventy`, themes, custom elements that load only where they're used, and the docs starter. Markdown picked up heading ids, footnotes, alerts and containers.
- **0.5: a trail buddy for your coding agent.** `ssqch init` writes an `AGENTS.md`, and `ssqch docs` answers from the version you installed, with no network. `ssqch footprint` and `budgets` tell you what a build weighs, archetypes became blueprints, and Jinja became the default.
- **0.6: a link checker with sharper eyes.** It reads feeds, the sitemap, `<meta>` URLs and JSON-LD too, and `ssqch serve --built` serves a build the way your host would.
- **0.7: lessons from a big move.** Folders full of links to other files copy now (one site we moved was missing 360 sample files), and `ssqch import eleventy` stopped taking advice from commented-out code.

## What you might need to change

Coming from 0.7 or earlier? Start with the 0.8 entry: it changes how you install ssqch. Each older release's breaking changes are in its own section of the changelog, with what a site had to change.
