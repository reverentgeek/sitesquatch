Your site has a second audience now: the coding agents your readers ask about it. This release gets a new site ready for them on day one, and gives you a ranger to walk the trail and point out what they'd trip on.

## The headlines

- **Go on patrol.** `ssqch patrol` reads any build, even another generator's, and tells you what an agent or a crawler would stumble over: a missing `llms.txt`, a page with two `<h1>`s, a Markdown copy that doesn't match its page.
- **Copies that say what the page says.** `llmsPlugin()` writes each page's Markdown after your templates run, links and all, so an agent reads what your visitors read.
- **Signs at the trailhead.** `robotsPlugin()` writes `robots.txt`, with AI crawlers sorted by what they're for: training, search, or a page someone asked about.
- **New sites start ready.** `ssqch init` turns on the sitemap, `robots.txt` and `llms.txt`, and every layout points agents at them.
- **Markdown on request.** Ask `ssqch serve` for `text/markdown`, and it answers with the page's copy.
- **A plugin for your old generator.** Moving from Jekyll, MkDocs, Hugo or VitePress? One plugin brings its habits along: `fromJekyll()`, `fromMkdocs()`, `fromHugo()` or `fromVitepress()`.

## What you might need to change

- **`ssqch inspect` is `ssqch trace`.** Same options, same JSON.
- **Another generator's options are plugins.** `jekyllTemplates`, `mkdocsTemplates`, and `markdown`'s `dialect`, `admonitions`, `tabs`, `shortcodes` and `htmlMarkdown` say which `from` plugin to add instead.
- **`sitemapPlugin()` needs your site's `url`.**
- **Gone:** `trimAfterFrontMatter`, `markdown.encodeUrls`, `containers: "markdown-it"` (it's what `true` does now), `sitemapPlugin({ format })`, `vuePlugin({ vitepress })`, `llmsPlugin({ hint })`, and `clean` in the config (`leaveNoTrace`). Each error says what to write instead.
- **Markdown copies moved:** a page's copy is `index.md` inside its folder now, and `markdownCopy(page.url)` gives the new address.
- **It's "First confirmed sighting." now**, if a script reads the first line of a build.

## And in 0.13.1

**21 November 2026.** A big site's map stays readable. Give a part of your site its own `llms.txt` with `paths: ["/docs/"]`, and the site's `llms.txt` points agents to it instead of listing every page. A section named "Optional" always comes last, for the links an agent can skip, and a file that grows past 50,000 characters moves its last pages there. `llmsTxt(page.url)` gives each page's layout the right map to point at, and `ssqch patrol` follows the trail into every one.

## And in 0.13.2

**22 November 2026.** Multilingual Liquid sites build about a third faster: a layout that looks up each language's pages with `where` now finds them in one pass instead of searching the whole list every time. A link with a bare `%` in it, like a spec's `#sec-%intrinsic%` anchor, comes out as a valid URL, and `ssqch diff` won't count that as a change. The starters point each page at the `llms.txt` that covers it.
