Common questions

Are the snacks really eaten by Sasquatch?

We can neither confirm nor deny. We can confirm that hikers eat them, and that the testimonials are real eyewitness accounts of the snacks.

How does the subscription work?

Pick a plan, pick monthly or yearly, and we mail a box on the first of each month. Skip a month or cancel any time from your account.

Do you ship to trail towns?

Yes. Thru-Hike members can send any box to a post office General Delivery address along the trail, and change it month to month.

What about allergies?

Every snack page lists its ingredients. Pine Cone Crunch, Moss Mix and Trailhead Toffee contain tree nuts; our kitchen also handles wheat and soy.

How long do the snacks keep?

Six months sealed, and about two weeks once the bag is open, if they last that long.
