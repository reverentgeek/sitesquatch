Privacy policy

Last changed January 1, 2026

We collect as little as we can, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • When you visit: a count of visits from a privacy-friendly analytics service. No cookies, and nothing that identifies you.
  • When you sign up or write to us: the email address and message you send, kept by our form provider.
  • When you subscribe: your shipping address and order history, kept by our payment provider.

What we do with it

We use your email address to send the trail reports you asked for, and to answer your messages. Every email has a link to stop them.
