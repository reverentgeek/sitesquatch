Upon Further Review, a Stump

I went back in daylight. It was a stump. A tall one, to be fair to me.

The description in this post's front matter is the page's meta description; posts without one get the site's, from src/_data/site.json.

I didn't take a picture, since it was a stump. When you have one worth showing, save it as src/img/stump.jpg and write ![The stump](/img/stump.jpg). The build turns it into a <picture> in three sizes, so it stays sharp on any screen. Whether it was sharp to begin with is between you and your camera.

A raccoon who keeps everything told me the trick for deploying: keep dist/img/ and .cache/ between builds, and the build never encodes the same picture twice. Raccoon's cache. Raccoon did not see anything tall.
