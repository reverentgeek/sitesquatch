Raiders of the Lost Path

January 22, 2026

A ranger came by with an old map of these notes. It pointed to a heading called "The tracks". I had renamed it "Where they lead" the week before. The ranger was not pleased, and neither was the build.

What the build said

The link checker (linksPlugin() in ssqch.config.js) follows every link inside the site after a production build. A link to a page that isn't there, or to a heading that isn't on it, fails the build and says where:

error: after_build hook failed: linksPlugin: the trail goes cold on 1 broken link:
  /posts/i-know-what-i-saw/ -> /posts/the-stump-has-moved/#the-tracks (no id "the-tracks" on that page)

The correction

A heading's id comes from its text, so renaming a heading moves its address. Point the link at the new one, #where-they-lead, or keep the old heading. In ssqch serve it's a warning, so you can keep writing while you look for it.
